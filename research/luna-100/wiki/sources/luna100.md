# Luna generation and revision experiment

Source: [experiment report](../../README.md), [manifest](../../output/manifest.json), and [round comparison](../../output/reports/round-comparison.json).

- [FACT luna100] One hundred accepted Luna originals remain unchanged. All changed in round 2, and eleven received further targeted edits in round 3.
- [FACT luna100] Three raw articles used the wrong source. Excluding them leaves 78 source-matched development drafts and 19 reserved drafts.
- [FACT luna100] The new density check matched 62/78 development drafts and 17/19 reserved drafts, with no matches in 50 reference bodies. Evaluative framing matched 6/78, 0/19 and 0/50 respectively.
- [FACT luna100] Default checker findings decreased from 1,449 to one. The remaining generic word-ban finding applies to a participant's surname, which was retained.
- [FACT luna100] Four additional first drafts had 29 findings compared with 70 in their corresponding original drafts. All four passed after a source-checked editing pass.
- [FACT luna100] Four final independent Luna high reviews checked 32 quoted claims and requested no corrections. Earlier low-reasoning reviews included false findings and incomplete coverage.
- [INFERENCE archive,luna100] Prompt guidance and contextual lint correction work together. A lower finding count does not establish author preference, and some reference distribution differences remain.
- [OPEN] No final example has been approved by the author. Published texts include collaborative editing and are not a pure authorship dataset.
