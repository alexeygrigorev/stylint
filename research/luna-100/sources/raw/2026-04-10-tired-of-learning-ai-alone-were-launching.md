## Why AI Shipping Labs?

We’re living in an era of rapid change, with fast-moving trends pushing us into a constant rush to learn new “must-learn” concepts, frameworks, and tools without a clear direction.

This is true in AI, especially for people who want to build with these technologies.

You may find yourself in situations like these:

* You already have a full-time job and still need to keep up. Meanwhile, your AI project keeps getting pushed to “next weekend.”
* You want to move into a new role that requires new tools and a broader skill set. You keep asking yourself where to start, what to focus on, what to ignore, and how to prepare.
* The flood of online resources doesn’t really help. You follow a tutorial, but still can’t build something on your own. Or you build a prototype, but it breaks in ways you don’t know how to fix.

In other words, you’re learning and building alone, without structure or support. And things stall.

If any of this resonates with you, you should be part of AI Shipping Labs.


It’s a community where you learn to build AI products by actually building and shipping them, with a clear plan, a group of practitioners alongside you, and regular check-ins to help you keep moving forward.

## What Happens Inside

When you join, you’ll get access to the Slack workspace, where you can connect with other members. As an early member, you also receive personal onboarding from me: a short call, a voice message exchange, or an asynchronous conversation. This is something I can offer while the community is still small.


Together, we’ll figure out where you are now and where you want to go. You’ll get a clear plan, and the community will help you work toward it through these activities:

* Accountability circles: mastermind groups with regular check-ins
* Building sessions: regular live calls where we build something together
* Group learning: going through courses and new materials together
* Career support: resume reviews, personal branding, and conference speaking
* Private discussions: interview prep, salary negotiations, and career moves
* Courses: mini-courses on specialized topics

### 1. Accountability Circles

I get asked for mentoring all the time. I usually say no because I don’t have the bandwidth, and one-on-one mentoring doesn’t scale.

But I’ve realized that what most people actually need isn’t a mentor. They need an accountability structure: a plan, a deadline, and a reason to show up prepared.

So, we’re creating mastermind groups that run in sprints with a clear start and end. We connect you with people at a similar level. You work through different problems and share your progress on a set deadline, similar to a company standup: you share what you did, what you plan to do, and what’s blocking you. The group helps you work through those blockers, and you learn a lot from each other in the process.

### 2. Building Sessions and Group Learning

We meet live once or twice a month to build something together for 1.5 to 2 hours. Sometimes I bring a problem I’m working on, sometimes a member does. The focus is on solving real problems together, not watching slides.

For group learning, members pick a topic, research it, and present their findings. We also go through new courses and materials together. Having a cohort makes it much easier to stay on track.

### 3. Career Support and Private Discussions

You can get help from the community with resume and LinkedIn reviews, including feedback from people who have been through the hiring process and know what works.

We also help with personal branding: learning in public, creating content on LinkedIn and X, and building a presence that opens up new opportunities.

Because it’s a private community, you can also talk about things you wouldn’t bring up publicly: what companies are asking in interviews right now, how to negotiate a better offer, or whether it’s time to leave your current job.

### 4. Courses

Mini-courses on specialized topics are also coming. They’ll be shorter, for about several weeks, and more focused than a full Buildcamp.

As an early member, you can help shape what gets built. Members vote on which courses to develop next.

Some ideas we already have:

* Python for AI Engineering: a comprehensive Python course for AI engineering preparation, but also useful anywhere Python is needed
* Spec-Driven Development for AI: how to write clear specifications and manage a team of AI agents
* Refactoring AI Slop: how to deal with AI-generated code that has too much boilerplate, too much defensive coding, and is hard to maintain

These are our current ideas, but what actually gets built will depend on what the community needs.

## Why Another Community?

Many of you know me from [DataTalks.Club](https://datatalks.club/). That’s not changing. The principle of free education for everyone stays the same. All of the [free Zoomcamps](https://datatalks.club/blog/guide-to-free-online-courses-at-datatalks-club.html) continue to exist, and I run one cohort of each every year.


Overview of all six free DataTalks.Club zoomcamp courses: Machine Learning, Data Engineering, MLOps, LLM, AI Dev Tools, and Stock Market Analytics

But for a while, I’ve wanted to build something different. The idea came together step by step.

First, I ran the [AI Engineering Buildcamp](https://maven.com/alexey-grigorev/from-rag-to-agents) on Maven, and the students showed me there’s real demand for structured, paid learning. They were engaged and wanted to keep going after the course ended.

Then I started [this personal newsletter](https://alexeyondata.substack.com/), a place to share what I learn and try every day, separate from DataTalks.Club.

That made me want a smaller group where I could go deeper: live sessions, solving problems together, exchanging experiences.

Over time, all of that came together naturally into a community.
