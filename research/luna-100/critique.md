# Critique

## Reflection

Passed. The 100 assignments and source-grouped split are explicit. Rules require differential evidence, counterexamples and tests. Iteration includes all drafts, with saved originals and source-fidelity review. Public interfaces remain compatible. The archive is read only.

## Decision review

Use 450–750 word standalone articles to support repeated generations without padding the shorter sources. Two treatments per source are correlated and will be reported as such. Compare trimmed article bodies rather than newsletter tails. Use a practical stopping test; absence of human labels prevents a preference claim but does not require stopping before implementation.

## Accepted risks

Repeated attempts can overfit the evaluation corpus. Hold-out grouping and final fresh review reduce but do not eliminate this. Published sources contain editing contributions. Technical facts are historical reconstructions rather than current recommendations. All artifacts stay local.

## Execution adjustments

The first bulk outputs copied and mechanically rewrapped source text. They were rejected and quarantined before analysis. All 100 accepted drafts were independently generated in bounded batches. Accepted 001–034 used Luna high;035–100 used Luna low.

The first source-fidelity audit read 069, so its source was removed from the held-out group. Previously unread source s17 replaced it before any held-out evaluation. Rule definitions remained frozen. The two treatments of each source stay together.

Three raw drafts contain 443,443 and 448 whitespace words, slightly below the nominal 450 target. They remain unchanged for provenance. For revisions, the accepted range is 400–750 when deletion of repeated material leaves a complete article. This avoids padding to meet an arbitrary word count. Article 006 demonstrated the problem: deleting its final repeated phone/laptop recap left a complete 436-word explanation.

The source alignment audit found three assignment failures in raw 037, 048 and 050. Original files remain preserved. Corrections use separate briefs and later article rounds. Source-matched calibration excludes those originals, including the reserved assignment that used a development source.

Low-reasoning reviews sometimes made false claims about the text or marked weak quote pairs as supported. Their outputs were treated as suggestions. Four final independent high-reasoning reviews verified 32 quoted claims against actual files and required no further corrections. This is sampled review, not exhaustive proof.

The final corpus has one retained finding because the existing `foster` word ban flags a participant's surname. Preserving attribution takes precedence over a clean count. Report tables and third-person research attribution also conflict with generic article-style checks; these explicit report-format exceptions are recorded separately.

The final phrase scan yielded ordinary constructions and technical terminology. No further high-confidence rule was justified. Paragraphs remain denser than the references, and account drafts may overuse first person. The outcome is a tested drafting and editing workflow with saved examples, not demonstrated human preference equivalence.
