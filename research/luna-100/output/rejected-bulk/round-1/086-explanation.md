Technical interview

Coding rounds generally fall into two categories: implementation and algorithm rounds.

You may need to design (in the “object-oriented design” sense of the word) the interaction between different components, define proper abstractions, and then implement only some parts of it.

The interviewer evaluates how you interact with the coding agent, how you review the code, test it, correct errors, and refine the implementation.
